Questions

What if no storm occurs? The coordination subscription still covers route staffing, scheduling, and dispatch readiness for the season; per-storm charges only occur for storms actually cleared.

What if there are many storms? Each completed storm is charged at your locked per-storm rate; coordination stays $49/mo Nov–Mar.

Who is my operator? A named roster operator assigned to your route, confirmed before the season.

What if I’m away? Tell the desk in advance; your route assignment and per-storm handling follow the Snow Terms.

How do I cancel? Per the Snow Terms, with cancellation instructions on the receipt — the season ends after the March payment in any case.
